Our Story

Where Every Cake
Tells a Story

Nestled in the heart of Lebanon, Pennsylvania, Cakes by Moraima & Cafe is where artistry meets flavor. Founded with a passion for creating edible masterpieces, Moraima brings warmth, creativity, and attention to detail to every single order.

Beyond our custom cakes, our cafe is a cozy neighborhood gem — serving all-day breakfast, freshly brewed artisan coffees, and delightful pastries baked fresh every morning. It's more than a bakery. It's a place where memories are made.
